About Me

I work as a full-time faculty member of the Department of Mathematics and Statistics of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, where I began teaching since 2007. I have taught various math courses ranging from Calculus to several major courses in the undergraduate programs offered in the Department.


I love to travel, learn languages, cook + eat, and code. I am also into digital typography and the science of fitness. Physics usually gets my attention, but I love teaching math.
